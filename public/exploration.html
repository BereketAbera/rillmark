One workspace. Three expressions.

A brief exploration of the same realistic application screen. Content and structure stay consistent so typography, surfaces, shape and spacing can do the talking. These are static visual studies.

Why Fieldnotes

Its character is present in an ordinary filter, a selected tab, and the quiet edge of a card. Warm surfaces and mineral ink give dense work a human quality; the small corner contrast adds recognition without consuming space. It scales naturally from a project table to settings and discovery. Signal is efficient but familiar; Terrace is welcoming but heavier at density.
